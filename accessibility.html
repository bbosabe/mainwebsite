Big Box Accessibility

Shopping Should Be Open to Everyone

We're committed to making our stores and website accessible to all customers — regardless of ability. Here's what we're doing and how to reach us if you need help.

Last updated: April 2026 BC & Alberta, Canada

Big Box Outlet Store is committed to ensuring that our stores, website, and customer communications are accessible to everyone — including people with visual, hearing, mobility, and cognitive disabilities.

We believe that a great shopping experience should be available to all customers. Whether you're visiting one of our 20 BC and Alberta locations or browsing bigboxoutletstore.ca, we want the experience to be clear, comfortable, and barrier-free.

If you encounter any barrier — on our website or in one of our stores — please tell us. We take every report seriously and will work to resolve it promptly.

Our website is built with the goal of conforming to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA — the internationally recognised standard for accessible web content and the benchmark used by most Canadian organisations.

WCAG 2.1 Level AA Our target conformance standard

WCAG 2.1 AA covers four core principles — content must be Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust. These principles guide how we design and build every page on our site.

We also aim to comply with the Accessible British Columbia Act (2021) and related provincial standards as they apply to our online presence and physical store locations.

We have implemented a range of features to improve the accessibility of our website for all visitors:

Keyboard Navigation
All interactive elements — menus, buttons, forms, and links — are reachable and operable using a keyboard alone.
Screen Reader Support
ARIA labels, roles, and live regions are used throughout the site so screen readers can accurately describe page content.
Colour Contrast
Text and interactive elements meet or exceed the WCAG AA contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.
Text Resizing
Content reflows correctly when text is resized up to 200% in the browser, with no loss of functionality or content.
Focus Indicators
Visible focus outlines appear on all interactive elements when navigating by keyboard, so you always know where you are on the page.
Responsive Layout
All pages adapt cleanly to different screen sizes and orientations — from desktop to mobile — without requiring horizontal scrolling.
Alternative Text
All images include descriptive alt text so that content is available to users who cannot see images.
Reduced Motion
Animations and transitions are suppressed for users who have enabled the "prefers-reduced-motion" setting on their device.

All Big Box Outlet Store locations are located in commercial properties that comply with applicable provincial building codes and accessibility regulations. While each location varies, we work to ensure that every store is welcoming to all customers.

What most of our locations offer

  • Accessible parking spaces near the main entrance
  • Wide aisles designed for mobility aid navigation
  • Accessible washroom facilities where available on-site
  • Staff who are available to assist with product location, carrying items, and checkout
  • Cart and trolley assistance available at all locations

Accessibility features vary by location. If you have specific requirements before visiting, please call ahead and our team will be happy to prepare for your arrival.

Find phone numbers and hours for your nearest store on our Store Locator page.

We are transparent about areas where our website does not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA, and we are actively working to address them:

  • Third-party embedded content — Some embedded maps and social media widgets may not fully conform to WCAG 2.1 AA. We are evaluating alternatives and workarounds for these components.
  • PDF documents — Some promotional flyers and documents linked from our site may not be fully accessible. We are working to ensure new documents meet accessibility standards going forward.
  • Older content — Some legacy pages and images may have incomplete alt text or insufficient contrast. We are auditing and correcting these on an ongoing basis.

If you encounter a specific barrier that is not listed here, please let us know and we will prioritise it.

Reporting a barrier

If you find something on our website or in one of our stores that creates a barrier for you, please tell us. We want to know, and we take every report seriously.

To report an accessibility issue or request an accommodation, please:

  • Email us: info@bbos.ca — include a description of the barrier and the page or location where you encountered it
  • Use our contact form: Contact Us — select "Accessibility" as the subject
  • Call your local store: Find phone numbers on our Store Locator

What to expect

We aim to acknowledge all accessibility reports within 2 business days and provide a resolution or interim accommodation within 10 business days. Complex issues may take longer, and we will keep you informed of our progress.

If you need content from our website in an alternative format — such as large print, plain text, or a different language — we are happy to help. Contact us and describe what you need.

Need Help or Found a Barrier?

We respond to all accessibility enquiries within 2 business days.
