Shopping & Products
5 questions
What products do you carry?

We carry appliances, furniture, clothing, electronics, outdoor gear, tools, kitchenware, and more — all at outlet prices. Inventory varies by location and changes weekly.

Do you have an online store?

We are primarily in-store only. Visit any of our 20 BC and Alberta locations to shop. Check our Weekly Deals page for current highlights.

When does new inventory arrive?

Fresh stock arrives every Thursday at all locations. Members get early access — sign up for the Rewards Club to stay ahead.

Do you carry brand-name products?

Yes! We carry major name brands across all categories. Our inventory comes from manufacturer overruns, end-of-season stock, and open-box items.

Can I check if a specific item is in stock?

Inventory changes rapidly. Your best bet is to call or visit your nearest store. Our team can check availability for you.

Pricing & Deals
4 questions
How are your prices so low?

We source directly from manufacturers and purchase overstock, clearance, and open-box inventory in volume. Low overhead plus direct sourcing = outlet prices.

What is your Best Price Promise?

If you find the exact same item at a lower price at a local competitor, show us proof and we'll beat it. In-store only, verifiable prices only. Learn more.

Do prices vary by location?

Yes, prices and availability may vary between locations due to different inventory.

Do you offer senior discounts?

Yes! We have regular Seniors Day events with special discounts. Ask in-store for the current schedule.

Returns & Exchanges
4 questions
What is your return policy?

Most items can be returned within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt, in original condition. See our full Return Policy for details.

Can I return items by mail?

We accept in-store returns only. Please bring your item and receipt to any Big Box Outlet location.

What if my item is defective?

Bring the item and receipt to any location. We'll exchange it or offer store credit at our team's discretion.

Can I exchange for a different size?

Yes, exchanges are available within 30 days with receipt, subject to availability.

Rewards Program
5 questions
Is the Rewards program free?

Yes — signing up is completely free. No credit card needed.

How do I earn points?

You earn 1 point for every $2 spent in-store at any Big Box Outlet Store location.

How do I redeem points?

Points can be redeemed for savings on future in-store purchases. Ask at the checkout counter.

Do points expire?

Points do not expire as long as your account is active.

How do I sign up?

Sign up in-store at any location, or visit bigboxoutletstore.ca to create your account online.

Store Information
4 questions
How many locations do you have?

We have 20 locations across BC and Alberta. Use our Store Locator to find the one nearest you.

What are your store hours?

Hours vary by location. Check the Store Locator for your nearest store's hours.

Do you have wheelchair-accessible stores?

Yes. All locations have accessible parking, wide aisles, and staff available to assist. See our Accessibility page for details.

Do you offer delivery?

We do not currently offer delivery. All purchases must be picked up in-store.

Gift Cards
3 questions
Do you sell gift cards?

Yes! Big Box Outlet Store gift cards are available for purchase in-store at all locations. Learn more about gift cards.

Can I check my gift card balance?

Visit any store location and a team member will check your balance for you.

Do gift cards expire?

No. Big Box Outlet Store gift cards do not expire.
