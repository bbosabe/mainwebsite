Our Story

Built on a Simple Promise

Big Box Outlet Store has been a fixture in Western Canada since 1985. What started as a single family-owned store has grown into 20 locations across British Columbia and Alberta — all united by one simple goal: to help people afford everyday life.

We buy overstock, open-box, and recertified merchandise directly from major brands and retailers, then pass those savings directly to you. No middlemen. No markups. Just honest prices on products you know and trust.

Every item we sell is personally inspected, tested, and cleared for resale by our team — so you get the quality you expect at a price that actually makes sense.
