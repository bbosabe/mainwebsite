Our Story

40 Years
in the
Making.

Helping People Afford Everyday Life — that's not a tagline. It's what we've been doing since 1984.

From a single Abbotsford warehouse to 20 locations across BC and Alberta, every year of this story was written by the customers who walked through our doors and left with more than they expected. Here's how we got here.

1984
One store. One big idea.

We opened our doors in Abbotsford with a simple belief: that everyday Canadians shouldn't have to choose between quality and affordability. Word spread faster than we expected. That first customer who walked out saving $200 on a fridge they couldn't otherwise afford — that was the whole point, right there. The community showed up, and we showed up right back.

1992
Word got out. Then the walls got bigger.

As demand grew, so did we. Our first expansion proved the model worked — that people across BC were looking for exactly what we offered. More locations meant more families in more communities finding real value on the brands they already trusted. Each new store brought the same promise: name brands, real savings, no runaround.

2008
A new name. The same promise.

Rebranding to Big Box Outlet Store wasn't a pivot — it was a declaration. We were committing to this model for the long haul. The name said it plainly: big brands, outlet prices, no pretense. Customers who'd known us for years barely skipped a beat. They already knew what we stood for — and so did we.

2025
Forty years. Still day one.

Twenty locations. Hundreds of thousands of customers who've made us part of their routine. We marked 40 years not with fanfare but with the same thing we've always had: great products at prices that actually move the needle for families. The numbers are real. The impact is more so. And we're just getting started.

What's Next

The next chapter
is already underway.

A fresh look. A bigger footprint. And the same belief we showed up with in 1984 — that quality shouldn't be a privilege. New stores are on the horizon, the Rewards program is growing, and we're finding new ways to bring even better value to the communities we call home. We're not done. We're just getting to the good part.

Who We Are

Big Box Outlet is a proudly Canadian retailer serving communities across BC and Alberta with quality brand-name finds at prices that make everyday life easier. We're here for people who want good products without the usual markup. No fuss, no gimmicks — just real value, helpful people, and a shopping experience that feels honest.

Since 1984, we've built something simple: a store where your dollar goes further. With 20 locations and counting, we're proud to be a part of the neighbourhoods we serve — not just a place to shop, but a place people trust.

What We Do

We source overstock, closeout, and end-of-line products from brands people already know and trust, then pass those savings along in store. That means everything from furniture and appliances to clothing, patio, and everyday home essentials. Because our inventory changes all the time, every visit brings something new to discover.

Our buyers work hard to find the best deals so you don't have to. When a brand has surplus stock or a product line ends, we step in — and you walk out with something great at a price that makes sense. It's a smarter way to shop, and it works.

Why We Do It

We started because we saw an opportunity to do retail differently. We believe smart shopping shouldn't mean sacrificing quality. By giving great products a second chance at reaching customers, we reduce waste and make quality accessible. Every dollar saved by our customers is proof that this model works.

At the end of the day, we're motivated by the people who walk through our doors. Families furnishing a first home. Seniors stretching a fixed income. Anyone who wants something real without paying a premium for it. That's who we do this for — and that's who keeps us going.

From school food programs and student initiatives to partnering with organizations like Habitat for Humanity, Big Box Outlet is proud to give back to the neighbourhoods we call home. Because caring about our communities isn't extra — it's who we are.
