Who We Are

Who We Are

Big Box Outlet is a proudly Canadian retailer serving communities across BC and Alberta with quality brand-name finds at prices that make everyday life easier. We're here for people who want good products without the usual markup. No fuss, no gimmicks — just real value, helpful people, and a shopping experience that feels honest.

What We Do

What We Do

We source overstock, closeout, and end-of-line products from brands people already know and trust, then pass those savings along in store. That means everything from furniture and appliances to clothing, patio, and everyday home essentials. Because our inventory changes all the time, every visit brings something new to discover.

Why We Do It

Why We Do It

We started because we saw an opportunity to do retail differently. We believe smart shopping shouldn't mean sacrificing quality. By giving great products a second chance at reaching customers, we reduce waste and make quality accessible. Every dollar saved by our customers is proof that this model works.

Community

We Show Up For
Our Communities

From school food programs and student initiatives to partnering with organizations like Habitat for Humanity, Big Box Outlet is proud to give back to the neighbourhoods we call home. Because caring about our communities isn't extra — it's who we are.
