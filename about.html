Helping People Afford Everyday Life — that's not a tagline. It's what we've been doing since 1984.

1984
One store. One big idea.

We opened our doors in Abbotsford with a simple belief: that everyday Canadians shouldn't have to choose between quality and affordability. Word spread faster than we expected. That first customer who walked out saving $200 on a fridge they couldn't otherwise afford — that was the whole point, right there. The Way We Work

What Makes Us Different

Who We Are
A proudly Canadian retailer, built for real people.

Big Box Outlet serves communities across BC and Alberta with quality brand-name finds at prices that make everyday life easier. No fuss, no gimmicks — just honest value and a shopping experience that feels like it was designed for you, not around you.

What We Do
We source smart so you can shop smart.

We buy overstock, closeout, and end-of-line products directly from the brands you already trust — and pass every dollar of savings straight to you. Furniture, appliances, clothing, patio, and more. Because our inventory turns constantly, every visit is different.

Why We Do It
Because smart shopping shouldn't require sacrifice.

We started because we believed retail could work differently. By giving great products a second chance at reaching customers, we reduce waste and make quality accessible. Every dollar saved by our customers is proof that this model works — and keeps us motivated to grow it.

Community

We Show Up For
Our Communities

From school food programs and student initiatives to partnering with organizations like Habitat for Humanity, Big Box Outlet is proud to give back to the neighbourhoods we call home. Because caring about our communities isn't extra — it's who we are.
