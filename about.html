Our Story

40 Years
in the
Making.

Helping People Afford Everyday Life — that's not a tagline. It's what we've been doing since 1985.

From a salvage contract in Surrey in 1985 to 20 locations across BC and Alberta, this story was built on a single belief — that Canadians deserve quality brands at prices that actually move the needle. Three rebrands, one acquisition, and four decades later, we're still just getting started.

1985
Borrowed money. A big idea. Everything to prove.

Co-founders Mark Funk and Todd Friesen — both 20 years old — borrowed from their fathers to acquire HB Distributors in Surrey, BC for $80,000. The company held salvage contracts with CN Rail and Overwaitea Food Group, buying and reselling damaged merchandise. It was Canada's earliest iteration of reverse logistics retail. The community showed up on day one, and the model proved itself almost immediately.

2006
MTF Price Matters — our first rebrand.

Two decades in, the company sharpened its identity. Operating as MTF Mainland Distributors, it had built a network of discount stores across the Lower Mainland and BC Interior — Vancouver, multiple Langley addresses, Abbotsford, Vernon. The rename to MTF Price Matters wasn't cosmetic. It signalled a shift from distributor identity to consumer-facing retailer. The promise was right in the name.

2014
Big Box Outlet Store is born.

The most significant transformation in the company's history. Rebranding to Big Box Outlet Store captured the scale, variety, and discount-warehouse energy the business had grown into. Electronics, furniture, clothing, hardware, appliances — all at up to 70% off retail. The 85,000-square-foot Langley distribution hub, where 192 warehouse staff processed returns via conveyor systems, was already the backbone of the chain. The name finally matched the scale.

2023
Into Alberta. Bigger than ever.

With 11 BC stores running strong — fuelled in part by a booming Costco returns partnership — Big Box Outlet Store expanded outside BC for the first time. The Edmonton, Alberta location opened February 1, 2023, becoming the 12th store overall. Western Canada's largest reverse logistics retailer now spanned two provinces, with over 200 employees and a model proven across nearly four decades.

2024
New ownership. Fastest growth yet.

Big Box Outlet Store was acquired by Wubs Investments — connected to the Wubs family behind Westland Insurance, one of Canada's largest brokerages — bringing new capital and leadership including President Jeff Driedger. In just over a year, the company went from 11 to nearly 20 locations: five new Alberta stores, three new BC stores. The same mission that started with $80,000 borrowed from two dads in Surrey was now reshaping how Canadians shop for big brands.

What's Next

20 locations today.
40 on the horizon.

With 15 BC locations and 6 Alberta locations already open, leadership has set a bold target of 40 stores across Western Canada — and eventual national expansion. New cities, new partnerships, and the same belief that started in a Surrey warehouse in 1985: that quality shouldn't be a privilege. The model works. We're just getting to the good part.

Who We Are

Big Box Outlet is a proudly Canadian retailer serving communities across BC and Alberta with quality brand-name finds at prices that make everyday life easier. We're here for people who want good products without the usual markup. No fuss, no gimmicks — just real value, helpful people, and a shopping experience that feels honest.

Since 1984, we've built something simple: a store where your dollar goes further. With 20 locations and counting, we're proud to be a part of the neighbourhoods we serve — not just a place to shop, but a place people trust.

What We Do

We source overstock, closeout, and end-of-line products from brands people already know and trust, then pass those savings along in store. That means everything from furniture and appliances to clothing, patio, and everyday home essentials. Because our inventory changes all the time, every visit brings something new to discover.

Our buyers work hard to find the best deals so you don't have to. When a brand has surplus stock or a product line ends, we step in — and you walk out with something great at a price that makes sense. It's a smarter way to shop, and it works.

Why We Do It

We started because we saw an opportunity to do retail differently. We believe smart shopping shouldn't mean sacrificing quality. By giving great products a second chance at reaching customers, we reduce waste and make quality accessible. Every dollar saved by our customers is proof that this model works.

At the end of the day, we're motivated by the people who walk through our doors. Families furnishing a first home. Seniors stretching a fixed income. Anyone who wants something real without paying a premium for it. That's who we do this for — and that's who keeps us going.
