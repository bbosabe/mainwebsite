Big Box Returns

Simple Returns. No Hassle.

Not happy with your purchase? We make returns easy. Here's everything you need to know.

Find a Store Contact Us
Section 1

Standard Return Window

  • 30 days from date of purchase with original receipt
  • Items must be in original condition with tags attached
  • In-store returns only — we do not accept returns by mail

Please keep your original receipt — it's required to process any return or exchange.

Section 2

What Can Be Returned

  • Most items in original, unused condition with receipt
  • Appliances (unopened/unused) within 30 days
  • Furniture and home goods within 30 days
  • Clothing and footwear with tags attached
  • Seasonal items within 30 days while season applies
Section 3

What Cannot Be Returned

  • Opened or used electronics and personal care items
  • Items without original receipt
  • Final sale / clearance items (marked as non-returnable at time of purchase)
  • Assembled furniture
  • Gift cards
Section 4

Exchanges

  • Exchange items for a different size, colour, or product of equal or lesser value
  • Exchanges follow the same conditions as returns
  • Exchanges handled in-store only
Section 5

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or is defective, bring it in with your receipt and we will exchange it or provide store credit at our discretion. We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

Contact us if you have questions before coming in — our team is happy to help you determine the best resolution before you make the trip.

Damaged or defective claims must still be accompanied by the original receipt and brought in within the 30-day window where possible.

Have a Question About Your Return?

Our team is happy to help. Bring your receipt and item to any Big Box Outlet Store location and we'll take care of you.
