How We Give Back

Our Community Initiatives

From food security to youth recreation, here's how Big Box Outlet makes a difference in every community we call home.

Food Bank Partnerships

We partner with local food banks across BC and Alberta to donate overstocked non-perishable goods. Surplus inventory that would otherwise go to waste instead goes to families in need — every week, at every location.

Youth Sports Sponsorships

Big Box Outlet is proud to sponsor youth sports leagues and community teams across our regions. We believe every kid deserves a chance to play — regardless of their family's budget. We cover registration fees, jerseys, and equipment needs.

Local Event Support

From community festivals to school fundraisers, we show up to support the events that make our neighbourhoods great. We donate prizes, sponsor booths, and send teams out to volunteer alongside the people who make each community special.

Shelter & Outreach Support

We donate household items, bedding, and furniture to shelters and outreach organizations serving vulnerable residents. When closeout items come in, we prioritize getting them to organizations doing the most good on the ground.

School Supply Drives

Each fall, our stores run school supply collection drives and donate directly to local elementary and secondary schools. Students shouldn't have to worry about showing up without the basics — and with our customers' help, many don't have to.

Senior Community Programs

We partner with senior centres and care homes to provide gently discounted household essentials and organize volunteer shopping trips. Seniors on fixed incomes deserve access to the same savings as everyone else.
