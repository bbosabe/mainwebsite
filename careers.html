Sales Associate
Now Hiring Part-Time & Full-Time Multiple BC & AB Locations Entry Level Welcome

You're the face of Big Box Outlet on the floor. Sales Associates help customers navigate our constantly-changing inventory, answer product questions, and create a shopping experience that keeps people coming back every Thursday. If you're energetic, friendly, and love finding a great deal as much as our customers do — this role is for you.

What You'll Do
  • Greet and assist customers on the floor
  • Help customers locate products and answer questions
  • Process sales transactions accurately and efficiently
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and well-merchandised floor
  • Assist with weekly inventory resets every Thursday
  • Promote the BBOS Rewards program to shoppers
What We're Looking For
  • Friendly, customer-focused attitude
  • Availability for some weekends and Thursdays
  • Ability to stand for extended periods and lift 30+ lbs
  • Previous retail or customer service experience (asset, not required)

Warehouse Associate — Langley Distribution Centre
Now Hiring Full-Time Langley, BC Physical Role

Our 85,000-square-foot Langley distribution centre is the engine behind the entire Big Box Outlet chain. Warehouse Associates process incoming returns and overstock from major retailers — including Canada's largest — via conveyor systems, re-certifying merchandise and routing it to our 20 retail locations. It's hands-on, fast-paced, and no two days look the same.

What You'll Do
  • Receive, inspect, and sort incoming merchandise
  • Operate conveyor systems and hand trucks safely
  • Re-certify and quality-check returned and overstock items
  • Prepare outbound shipments to retail locations
  • Maintain a safe, organized warehouse environment
  • Meet daily throughput targets as a team
What We're Looking For
  • Ability to lift up to 50 lbs regularly
  • Comfortable on your feet for full shifts
  • Reliable, punctual, and team-oriented
  • Forklift certification is an asset (training available)

Store Manager
Leadership Role Full-Time BC & Alberta Locations Experience Required

As Store Manager, you run the show. You lead a team of retail staff, drive sales performance, manage daily operations, and make sure every customer walks out feeling like they won. With Big Box Outlet growing to 40+ locations, Store Managers have real opportunities to advance and grow alongside the company.

What You'll Do
  • Lead, schedule, and develop your retail team
  • Drive store KPIs: sales, shrinkage, customer satisfaction
  • Oversee weekly inventory resets and merchandising
  • Manage opening/closing procedures and cash
  • Hire and onboard new team members
  • Liaise with the distribution centre on stock needs
What We're Looking For
  • 3+ years retail management experience
  • Strong leadership and communication skills
  • Comfortable with high-volume, fast-changing inventory
  • Proven track record of meeting or exceeding sales targets

Delivery & Logistics Coordinator
Now Hiring Full-Time Langley, BC Driver's Licence Required

Keep the supply chain moving. As a Delivery & Logistics Coordinator, you'll help manage outbound shipments from our Langley distribution centre to our retail network. We run two trucks for Lower Mainland stores and a semi-trailer for Interior locations — and this role keeps those routes running like clockwork.

What You'll Do
  • Coordinate outbound truck routes to retail locations
  • Schedule pickups and deliveries across BC & Alberta
  • Track shipments and resolve logistics issues
  • Communicate with store teams on incoming stock
  • Assist with loading/unloading at the DC as needed
  • Maintain accurate shipping records and manifests
What We're Looking For
  • Valid BC driver's licence (Class 5 minimum)
  • Experience in logistics, dispatch, or supply chain
  • Strong organizational and communication skills
  • Comfortable with early starts and dynamic schedules

Buyer / Purchasing Associate
Key Role Full-Time Abbotsford HQ Experience Required

The deal starts with you. As a Buyer, you source the overstock, closeout, and open-box merchandise that fills our shelves every Thursday. You'll build relationships with brand reps and liquidators, negotiate pricing, and identify the products our customers love — whether that's a KitchenAid for $60 or a sectional for $400. Your instincts drive what 20 stores sell.

What You'll Do
  • Source overstock and closeout merchandise from major brands
  • Negotiate pricing with suppliers and liquidators
  • Analyse sell-through data to guide future purchases
  • Manage vendor relationships and build new supply channels
  • Collaborate with distribution and store teams on product routing
  • Stay ahead of consumer trends in electronics, furniture, and apparel
What We're Looking For
  • 2+ years of retail buying or procurement experience
  • Strong negotiation and vendor relationship skills
  • Analytically minded — comfortable with spreadsheets and sell-through data
  • Passion for finding a deal and a genuine love for retail
