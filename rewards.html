Got Questions?

FAQs

What purchases do I receive points on?
You receive points on every item you purchase except for gift cards.
Do I need a BBO Rewards card?
No. Just use your email or phone number at checkout — no physical card required.
How do I redeem my points?
Points are applied automatically at checkout. Just let the cashier know you're a Rewards member.
Can I use my points at any location?
Yes — your Rewards points work at all 20 Big Box Outlet Store locations across BC and Alberta.
