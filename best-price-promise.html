The Details
What qualifies for the Best Price Promise?
✓
What's Included
  • ✓ Same product, same model/SKU
  • ✓ Competitor must be a local physical retail store
  • ✓ Item must be in stock at both locations
  • ✓ Advertised retail price (not clearance or liquidation)
  • ✓ Price must be current and verifiable
✗
What's Excluded
  • ✗ Online-only prices (Amazon, eBay, etc.)
  • ✗ Limited-time flash sales under 24 hours
  • ✗ Liquidation or going-out-of-business sales
  • ✗ Open-box or used item prices
  • ✗ Prices that cannot be verified by our team
