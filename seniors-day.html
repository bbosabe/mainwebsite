Eligibility

Who Qualifies & How It Works

Eligibility Requirements
  • Must be a Big Box Rewards member — free to join in-store or online
  • Must be 65 years of age or older at the time of purchase
  • Valid government-issued photo ID required at checkout (driver's licence, provincial ID, or passport)
  • In-store purchases only — not applicable to online orders
  • 20% discount applies to your entire purchase, up to a maximum of $100 savings per visit
  • One discount per member per visit; cannot be combined with other promotions
Where & When
  • Seniors Day runs every week — check with your local store for the specific day at that location
  • Available at all 20 Big Box Outlet locations across British Columbia and Alberta
  • No appointment needed — valid during regular store hours
  • Thursday is new-inventory day — many members visit then for the freshest selection at the best prices

Common Questions

Seniors Day FAQ

Is Seniors Day every week?

Yes — Seniors Day is a recurring weekly event at all 20 of our BC and Alberta locations. Sign up for email alerts above and we'll remind you before each Seniors Day.

Do I need a Rewards membership?

Yes. The 20% discount is exclusive to Big Box Rewards members. Membership is completely free — sign up in the form above or at the service desk at any store location.

Is there a minimum purchase?

No minimum required. The 20% discount applies to your entire in-store purchase. The maximum discount is $100 per visit — so on a $500 purchase, you save the full $100.

Can I use my discount the same day I sign up?

Yes — your Rewards membership is active immediately when you sign up in-store. If signing up online, bring your confirmation email and valid ID on your next Seniors Day visit.

What ID is accepted?

Any valid government-issued photo ID showing your date of birth is accepted — BC driver's licence, BC Services Card, provincial ID card, or Canadian passport.

Can I combine it with other promotions?

The Seniors Day discount generally cannot be combined with other promotions or coupons unless specifically stated. Ask your local store team for details on current deals.
