Who Qualifies & How It Works

Eligibility Requirements
  • Must be a Big Box Rewards member (free to join in-store or online)
  • Must be 65 years of age or older at the time of purchase
  • Valid government-issued ID required at checkout (driver's licence, passport, or provincial ID)
  • In-store purchases only — discount not applicable to online orders
  • Discount applies to your entire purchase; maximum savings of $100 per visit
  • Limit one Seniors Day discount per member per visit
  • Cannot be combined with other promotions or coupons unless otherwise noted
Where & When
  • Seniors Day runs every week — check with your local store for the exact day at your location

Common Questions

Seniors Day FAQ

Is Seniors Day every week?

Yes — Seniors Day is a recurring weekly event at all 20 of our BC and Alberta locations. Sign up for email alerts and we'll remind you each week before the day arrives.

Do I need a Rewards membership?

Yes. The 20% Seniors Day discount is exclusive to Big Box Rewards members. Membership is completely free — sign up above, or at the service desk at any store location.

Is there a minimum purchase amount?

No minimum required. The 20% discount applies to your entire in-store purchase. The maximum savings per visit is $100 — so on a $500 purchase, you'd save the full $100.

Can I use my discount on the same day I sign up?

Yes — your Rewards membership is active immediately upon sign-up in-store. If signing up online, bring your confirmation email and a valid ID on your next Seniors Day visit.

What ID is accepted?

Any valid government-issued photo ID showing your date of birth is accepted — driver's licence, BC Services Card, provincial ID card, or Canadian passport.

Can I use the discount more than once per week?

The discount applies once per visit per member. You're welcome to visit a store on any Seniors Day — if you visit multiple times on different days, each qualifying visit is eligible.

Can I use the Seniors Day discount with other promotions?

The Seniors Day discount cannot be combined with other promotions, coupons, or sale prices unless specifically stated. Ask your local store team for details on current promotions.

Are there any excluded products?

The 20% Seniors Day discount applies to your entire eligible in-store purchase. Certain vendor restrictions may occasionally apply — store staff will let you know at checkout if any item is excluded.
