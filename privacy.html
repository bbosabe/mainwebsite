Big Box Privacy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

We're transparent about how we collect and use your information. Tap any section below to read the details — written in plain language, not legalese.

Last updated: April 2026 Effective immediately BC & Alberta, Canada

When you make a purchase in one of our stores or interact with our website, we may collect personal information you provide — such as your name, email address, mailing address, and phone number. This information is used only to process your transaction, communicate about your order, and provide after-sale support.

When you browse bigboxoutletstore.ca, we automatically receive your device's IP address. This helps us understand your browser type and operating system so we can improve your experience and ensure the site works correctly for all visitors.

Email Marketing

With your explicit permission, we may send you emails about new arrivals, exclusive member deals, and weekly flyers. You will only receive marketing emails if you have actively opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.

How we obtain your consent

When you provide personal information to complete a transaction — such as to verify a payment, place an order, arrange delivery, or process a return — we assume your consent to collect and use that information for that specific purpose only.

If we need your personal information for a secondary reason (such as marketing communications), we will ask for your express consent directly or give you a clear option to decline.

Withdrawing your consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time. To stop receiving marketing communications, or to request that we stop using your information for any purpose, please contact us or email us at info@bbos.ca and we will respond promptly.

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Full stop.

We may disclose your information only when required by law, to comply with a court order or regulatory request, or if disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Big Box Outlet Store, our customers, or others.

Your personal information will never be sold to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial gain.

We work with trusted third-party service providers to operate our website and deliver our services. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their specific function and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

Some providers — such as payment processors — may be located in jurisdictions outside Canada. As a result, your information may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

For example: if you're in Canada and your transaction is processed by a payment gateway in the United States, that information may be subject to U.S. laws including the USA PATRIOT Act.

External Links

Once you leave our website or are redirected to a third-party site, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites before sharing personal information.

Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. It collects anonymised usage data only. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Protecting your personal information is a priority. We follow industry best practices and take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of your data.

Any payment card information you provide is encrypted using SSL (Secure Socket Layer) technology and stored with AES-256 encryption. While no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, we comply with all applicable PCI-DSS requirements and apply additional safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we handle.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help the site function correctly, remember your preferences, and give us insight into how people use our site.

Types of cookies we use

Necessary
Core site functionality — security, session management, and accessibility. The site cannot work without these.
Preferences
Store your settings so the site remembers things like your selected region between visits.
Analytics
Help us understand visitor behaviour — traffic sources, popular pages, and navigation paths. Data is reported anonymously.
Marketing
Help us show relevant content and measure the performance of our promotions. Only set with your consent.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Note that some features — such as region preferences — may not work correctly if cookies are disabled. Learn more at allaboutcookies.org.

By using this website, you confirm that you are at least the age of majority in your province or territory of residence. If you are a minor, you confirm that you have obtained the consent of your parent or legal guardian to use this site.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of majority without appropriate parental or guardian consent. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

We may update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. We encourage you to check this page periodically. Any changes take effect immediately upon being posted to the website.

If we make material changes — changes that significantly affect how we collect, use, or share your information — we will post a prominent notice on this page so you are aware of what has changed and when.

If Big Box Outlet Store is acquired by or merged with another company, your information may be transferred to the new owners so that we may continue to offer our products and services. You will be notified of any such change via a notice on our website.

Questions or Concerns?

To access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to raise a privacy concern, please reach out — we aim to respond within 2 business days.

Email: info@bbos.ca
