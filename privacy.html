Your Privacy Matters to Us

We're committed to being transparent about how we collect and use your information. This policy explains your rights and how Big Box Outlet Store protects your personal data.

Section 1

Information We Collect

When you purchase something from our store, we collect the personal information you provide to complete your transaction — such as your name, address, and email address. This is necessary to process your order and provide after-sale support.

When you browse bigboxoutletstore.ca, we automatically receive your device's IP address. This helps us understand your browser type and operating system so we can improve your browsing experience and ensure our website works correctly for all visitors.

Email Marketing

With your explicit permission, we may send you emails about new arrivals, exclusive member deals, weekly flyers, and other updates from Big Box Outlet Store. You will only receive marketing emails if you have actively opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Section 2

Consent

How we obtain your consent

When you provide personal information to complete a transaction — such as to verify a payment, place an order, arrange delivery, or process a return — we assume your consent to collect and use that information for that specific purpose only.

If we need your personal information for a secondary reason (such as marketing communications), we will either ask for your express consent directly or provide you with a clear opportunity to decline.

Withdrawing your consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time. To stop receiving communications from us, or to request that we stop collecting, using, or disclosing your information, please contact our Privacy Compliance Officer:

Email: info@bigboxoutletstore.ca
Mail: Big Box Outlet Store — Privacy Compliance Officer
101-1777 Clearbrook Rd, Abbotsford, BC  V2T 5X5
Section 3

Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may disclose your personal information only when required by law, when necessary to comply with a court order or regulatory request, or if you have violated our Terms of Use and disclosure is required to protect the rights, property, or safety of Big Box Outlet Store, our customers, or others.

Section 4

Third-Party Services

We work with third-party service providers to help operate our website and deliver our services. These providers are only given the information necessary to perform their specific function and are prohibited from using it for other purposes.

Certain third-party providers — such as payment processors — have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your information. We encourage you to read those policies. Note that some providers may be located in jurisdictions outside Canada, and your information may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

For example: if you are in Canada and your transaction is processed by a payment gateway in the United States, that information may be subject to U.S. laws, including the Patriot Act.

External Links

Once you leave bigboxoutletstore.ca or are redirected to a third-party website, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites and encourage you to review their policies before sharing any personal information.

Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to help us understand who visits our site, which pages are most popular, and how people navigate through our content. Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Section 5

Security

Protecting your personal information is a priority. We follow industry best practices and take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of your data.

Any credit card information you provide is encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology and stored with AES-256 encryption. While no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, we comply with all PCI-DSS requirements and apply additional industry-accepted safeguards.

Section 6

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help the site function correctly, remember your preferences, and give us insight into how our site is used. Below is a breakdown of the cookies we use and why.

Cookie Types

Necessary
Core functionality including security, network management, and accessibility. The website cannot function properly without these.
Preferences
Store your settings and preferences to personalize how the website looks and behaves for you.
Marketing
Help us show you relevant content, remember browsing history, and provide product recommendations.
Analytics
Help us understand visitor behaviour — number of visitors, traffic sources, and page navigation. Data is reported anonymously.

Specific Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Type Duration Purpose
_session_id Necessary Session Stores session information such as your referral source and landing page.
cart Necessary 2 weeks Stores the contents of your shopping cart between visits.
_secure_session_id Necessary Session A secure variant of the session cookie used for encrypted communication.
storefront_digest Necessary Indefinite Used to verify whether the current visitor is authorized to access password-protected areas of the site.
PREF Analytics Short-term Set by Google. Tracks site visits and traffic sources to help us understand how visitors find our store.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. However, doing so may affect how certain features of our website function. Visit allaboutcookies.org to learn more about managing cookies.

Section 7

Age of Consent

By using this website, you confirm that you are at least the age of majority in your province or territory of residence. If you are a minor, you confirm that you have the consent of your parent or legal guardian to use this site.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of majority without appropriate parental or guardian consent.

Section 8

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Any changes take effect immediately upon being posted to the website.

If we make material changes — changes that significantly affect how we collect, use, or share your information — we will provide a prominent notice on this page so you are aware of what has changed.

If Big Box Outlet Store is acquired by or merged with another company, your information may be transferred to the new owners so that we can continue to provide you with our products and services. We will notify you of any such change via a notice on our website.
