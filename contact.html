Common Questions

We've Got Answers

What's your return policy?
Most items can be returned within 30 days with a receipt. Visit your nearest store for details — our team will be happy to help.
Do you sell online?
We're primarily in-store only. Check our weekly flyer for current deals and visit us at any of our 20 BC and Alberta locations.
How do I join Rewards?
Sign up for free at any Big Box Outlet Store location or online at bigboxoutletstore.ca. It takes less than a minute and there's no cost to join.
When does new stock arrive?
Fresh inventory drops every Thursday. Rewards Club members get early access — sign up to be first in line for the best deals.
